Poly Haven PBR surface maps used by the Aurelle exterior builder

Assets:
- asphalt_02 (2K): https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02
- granular_concrete (2K): https://polyhaven.com/a/granular_concrete
License: CC0 1.0 Universal; https://polyhaven.com/license

Base color, DirectX normal, and roughness maps were downloaded from Poly Haven. The *_metallic_smoothness.png maps are derived packing textures: black RGB (non-metallic) and inverse roughness in alpha for Unity URP Lit's metallic/gloss map channel.
